# First-time setup

About 60 seconds, the first time you connect a store.

---

> You: "set me up"

Reading your store quickly so I have something to go on…

Here's what I learned:

- Plan: Shopify, USD, MST timezone, US-based.
- About 120 products across 14 collections.
- ~80 orders in the last 30 days.
- Returning customers: ~28% (last 90 days).
- 1 location. All sampled products track inventory.

A few quick questions so I can be useful:

1. Who am I talking to, and what's your role? (e.g. "Operator A, founder")
2. Roughly what stage is the store in? (just-launched / under-10k-mo / 10k-100k / 100k-1m / 1m+)
3. When you ask me to change something — what's your default?
   - (a) Always show me first, always wait for "yes," even on small things.
   - (b) Show me first; if I say "do it" assume yes.
   - (c) Big or bulk changes: always confirm. Small ones: just do it.
4. Want a Monday-morning brief each week? (yes-Monday / yes-other-day / no)

> You: "1) Operator A, founder. 2) 10k-100k. 3) a. 4) yes-Monday."

Here's what I'll save:

- Operator A, founder.
- Stage: 10k-100k. Model: DTC, US-only, ~120 SKUs, single location.
- Write defaults: always preview, always wait for "yes."
- Weekly brief: Monday.

Save this to your store? Type "yes" to save, "edit" to change something, "no" to skip and use defaults.

(Heads-up: after you say yes, Shopify itself will ask you to confirm the save once more — that's normal.)

> You: "yes"

Saved. You're all set. What do you want to work on?

---

— under the hood —

The setup uses the `onboarding/first-run` skill. It runs a read-only discovery sweep, asks a small number of questions, previews what it'll save, and writes your profile to your store so the bundle remembers you next time. You can refresh it any time with "refresh my profile."
