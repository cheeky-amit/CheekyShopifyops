# Inventory snapshot

Current stock — for one product or a cohort — broken down by variant and location.

> "What's my stock?"
> "Inventory snapshot for Product A."
> "Stock for vendor:Vendor A."

## Two modes

- **Single product:** variant × location grid. Useful when triaging "do I have any X left?"
- **Cohort:** per-product totals with flags for "out at one location" or "no stock anywhere."

## Untracked inventory

Products with inventory tracking turned off don't show real numbers. They're surfaced separately as "tracking off."
