# Top customers by spend

A ranked list of your top spenders or repeat buyers, so you can see who actually pays your bills.

## What you can say

> "Top customers."
> "Best 20 spenders."
> "Repeat buyers in Canada."

## What you'll see back

A ranked list. Each row shows the customer, their lifetime spend, how many orders they've placed, and the country they're in. By default you'll see the top 10 — ask for more if you need a deeper list.

## What it won't do

- Won't email these customers — there's no email tool in this bundle.
- Won't tag or un-tag them in bulk. That's a write skill not in v1.
- Won't show spend from refunded orders any differently — totals are gross.

## Privacy note

Names are shown abbreviated by default — first name plus last initial, like "Customer A B." That keeps the list scannable without splashing every customer's full identity on screen. To see one specific customer in full (full name, email, address), ask for them by name or position — for example, "show Customer A B." or "show #1."

## Settings you can change

- **How many** (default: 10). Ask for any number — "top 20," "top 50."
- **Group of customers** (default: everyone). Filter by country, tag, or order count — e.g. "repeat buyers in Canada."

## Want to see under the hood

It pulls customers ordered by lifetime spend, applies your filter, and abbreviates names for the summary view. The full record stays accessible if you ask for one specifically.
