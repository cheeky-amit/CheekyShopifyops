# New-store previews

Generate up to three storefront mockups from a short brand description. Pick the one you like and sign up — Shopify creates a brand-new store with that look and matching products pre-installed.

## What you can say

> "Show me what a stoneware mugs store could look like."
> "Generate previews for a hiking gear shop, urban 25-35, earthy aesthetic."
> "Preview a new store from a description."

## Important — these are for new stores only

These previews **can only be claimed as brand-new stores.** They cannot be applied to a storefront you already have. The skill confirms this up front so nobody loses time generating mockups they can't use. If you already run a Shopify store and want to redesign it, this isn't the right path.

## What it asks

Three short descriptors — these are the inputs the generator needs to make the previews feel right:

- **Product or service** — what you'd sell.
- **Target audience** — who would buy.
- **Brand style** — visual mood, palette, references.

The skill compresses what you say into the keyword form the generator wants. It won't invent attributes you didn't mention.

## What you'll see back

After confirmation, generation runs for around 3 minutes. When it's done, three preview options appear inline. Each one has a signup link — clicking it creates a brand-new Shopify store with that theme and matching products pre-installed. You can also brainstorm different directions and re-run; nothing is committed until you click a signup link.

## What it won't do

- Won't apply previews to an existing store. Brand-new stores only.
- Won't sign you up automatically. The signup link is the moment of commitment, and that's on you to click.
- Won't store the descriptors anywhere. Each run is fresh.

## Want to see under the hood

It checks you're starting a new brand, asks the three descriptor questions, compresses your answers into the keyword form the generator expects, and kicks off generation. The host renders the three previews when ready.
