# Generate a new-store preview

Generate up to 3 storefront previews for a brand-new store idea.

> "Show me what a stoneware mugs store could look like."
> "Generate previews for a hiking gear shop, urban 25-35, earthy aesthetic."

## Important constraint

Previews can only be claimed as **brand-new** stores. They cannot be applied to an existing storefront. The skill confirms this up front.

## What it asks

Three short descriptors:
- **Product or service** — what you'd sell.
- **Target audience** — who would buy.
- **Brand style** — visual mood, palette, references.

The skill compresses what you say into the keyword form the generator wants. It won't invent attributes you didn't mention.

## What happens next

The generator takes ~3 minutes. Three preview options appear in the host. Each has a signup link that creates a brand-new Shopify store with that theme and matching products pre-installed.
