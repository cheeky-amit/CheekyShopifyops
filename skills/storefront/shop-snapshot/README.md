# Shop snapshot

A one-glance "where am I" — what store you're on, what plan, where it's based, how big the catalog is, and what the last week looked like. Useful right after switching stores or at the start of a session.

## What you can say

> "Shop snapshot."
> "What plan am I on?"
> "Show me my store."

## What you'll see back

A short profile: domain, plan, currency, timezone, and country at the top; product and collection counts in the middle; orders and total sales for the last 7 days at the bottom; and a few sample asks you can copy-paste to keep going.

## What it won't do

- Won't list specific products, customers, or orders. For that, ask for what you want directly (e.g. "today's pulse," "stuck orders," "low stock").
- Won't change anything — read-only.
- Won't return billing details or app subscriptions.

## Settings you can change

- **Time window** (default: last 7 days). Ask for a different window if you want the bottom block over 30 days, year-to-date, etc.

## Want to see under the hood

It reads the shop profile, counts products and collections, and totals orders and sales over the last 7 days.
