# New vs returning customer mix

How much of your business comes from repeat customers, and is that share growing?

> "New vs returning customers."
> "Customer retention."
> "Returning customer rate over time."

## What it returns

- Returning-customer rate (this period vs prior).
- New vs returning customer counts.
- Time-series chart for the trend.
- A short interpretation.

## Caveats

- Stores under ~90 days of order history have unstable retention metrics — too small a denominator. The skill flags this.
- Subscription products skew the metric upward. The skill asks if the merchant runs subscriptions when the rate looks unusually high.
