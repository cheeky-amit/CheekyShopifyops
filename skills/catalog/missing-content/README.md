# Find products missing content

Products without descriptions, images, alt text, vendor, type, or tags.

> "Audit my product content."
> "Products missing description."
> "What products are incomplete?"

## Why this matters

Storefront polish + SEO. A product without an image hurts conversion. Without alt text, hurts accessibility and SEO. Without tags, hurts discoverability and collection rules.

## What this skill won't do

- Auto-write missing content. That's a separate write skill (a future `catalog.content-fix` could do AI-suggested descriptions, but it's not in v1 — content writes need careful preview/confirm).
