# Find products missing content

Spot products without descriptions, images, alt text, vendor, type, or tags — the gaps that quietly hurt your storefront.

## What you can say

> "Audit my product content."
> "Which products are missing descriptions?"
> "What products are incomplete?"

## What you'll see back

You'll get a summary of how many products are missing each kind of content (description, image, alt text, vendor, type, tags), a list of the worst offenders (products missing the most fields), and quick-win callouts — for example, products with no image at all, which look broken on the storefront.

## What it won't do

- Won't auto-write descriptions or fill in fields. Content writes need careful review and live in a separate skill.
- Won't change anything on its own — this is a read-only audit.

## Want to see under the hood

It scans your products, checks each one for empty fields, and groups the gaps so you can see which fixes will move the needle (missing image > missing description > missing tags). When you want to fix one, you can ask — for example, "fix content on Product A" — and a separate write skill takes over.
